# SIWES-PROJECT.
Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) is a skill training program established by the Industrial Training Fund(ITF) in  the year 1973 to expose and prepare students of higher institutions for the Industrial Work situation they are likely to meet after Graduation. It also give student availability to make connections with other school students and strong connections with Industrial Managers and Directors.

IMPORTANCE OF SIWES
1. Bridges the gap between theory and practice: SIWES enables students to apply classroom knowledge to real-life work situations.
2. Develops practical skills: Students gain hands-on experience with tools, equipment, and techniques used in their profession.
3. Improves professional competence: SIWES helps students develop problem-solving, communication, teamwork, and time-management skills.
4. Builds confidence: Practical experience increases students' confidence in performing job-related tasks.
5. Prepares students for future careers: The programme equips students with the knowledge and experience needed for a smooth transition from school to the workplace.
